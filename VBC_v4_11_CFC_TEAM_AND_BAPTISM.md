# VBC Member Care v4.11

Apply `supabase/032_cfc_team_certificates_baptism.sql` after migration 031 in Supabase SQL Editor, then deploy v4.11. No new environment variables; keep your existing CFC PIN secret and current accounts. Do not reapply migration 031 after 032.

- **Follow Through → CFC Leader account:** Create up to five active, individually named accounts. Each has its own PIN. Existing accounts retain their PINs. Deactivate an unused account to free a slot.
- **Participants → Add participant:** Find a confirmed church member by name or, when contact consent permits, phone. Select a cohort and enrol. Established legacy members can be enrolled without filling in a joining date. No duplicate member is created.
- **Classes:** Filter by cohort, choose a class session, and find a participant in that session. **Participants → View progress** shows the person's history across cohorts. Seven distinct classes are still required for completion.
- **CFC certificates:** On a participant’s progress page, record the certificate number and issue date after approved CFC completion. Mark it ready, then record collection. The signed-in staff member is recorded as the person who handed it over.
- **Baptism:** Register an existing confirmed member, schedule water baptism, then record the actual baptism date. Issue and track its certificate independently of CFC. This register does not require CFC completion.
